### 1. Escape From Playtime — a new co-op horror game

Mob Entertainment announced a new game set in the Poppy Playtime universe on August 26. Unlike the main series, where the player explores an abandoned factory from a first-person perspective, this time the Smiling Critters themselves — colorful toy animals — are the main characters.

The game supports 1–4 players. The characters will explore the Playtime Co. factory, collect materials, complete tasks for a mysterious voice, and hide from dangerous toys at the same time.

**Early Access is scheduled for October 21.** An open playtest also took place earlier this month.

### 2. FNAF × Fortnite — a major Halloween collaboration

Epic Games launched Fortnitemares 2026 on October 1, bringing Five Nights at Freddy's characters into Fortnite.

The main highlight of the collaboration is the arrival of the classic animatronics Freddy Fazbear, Bonnie, Chica, and Foxy. Their outfits can show battle damage.

Animatronic bosses were also added to the game. After defeating one, a player can temporarily take control of it and hunt other players.

This means the FNAF characters are not just cosmetic skins — they actively participate in the event.

### 4. Minecraft Dungeons II — a new official game

Minecraft Dungeons II is a new adventure game set in the Minecraft universe, released on September 29, 2026.

Unlike regular Minecraft, where players build homes and explore the world, the main focus here is fighting monsters, hunting for treasure, and defeating dangerous bosses.

In the story, a group of heroes sets out to save the world from new villains — a council of evil beings known as Illagers. The game introduces new monsters, unusual locations, and a mysterious dimension called The Sift, filled with fantastical creatures.

### 5. The Sift — Minecraft's fourth dimension

At Minecraft Live 2026, Mojang introduced The Sift, a dimension that first appeared in Minecraft Dungeons II.

Unlike older dimensions, The Sift is an unusual space filled with bright vegetation, souls, mysterious creatures, and altered environmental physics.

Mojang also announced that the dimension will arrive in Minecraft Java and Bedrock Edition in 2027.

It is the first newly announced dimension for the main game in more than 14 years.

### 9. *🔥* Minecraft — GroxMC × Froggy Dude (Frox / Groggy)

GroxMC and Froggy Dude are characters from Minecraft videos made by two popular YouTubers. Grox portrays himself as a cruel, self-absorbed ruler who forces Minecraft villagers to work for him, pay taxes, and obey absurd commands. Froggy Dude, by contrast, plays a rebellious hero trying to survive in Grox’s world and resist his rule.

Their rivalry became the basis for the fan ship **Frox**.

GroxMC has several recognizable catchphrases that fans use in memes, fan animations, and shipping content.

The most notable ones are:

1. “AYO CHILL!!!” — his most recognizable phrase, usually shouted when something unexpected, dangerous, or awkward happens.
2. “Aight bet!” — used when he accepts a challenge or agrees to some ridiculous idea.
3. “Let's get LITTT!!” — an excited shout before fun or chaos begins.
4. “Dear God...” — a reaction to something completely absurd.

### 10. *🔥* Helluva Boss — Season 3

Season 3 of Helluva Boss is scheduled to premiere on October 14.

It is an adult animated series created by Vivienne Medrano about I.M.P., a small company of demons who work as hired killers and take jobs in the human world.

The story centers on Blitzø, the head of the company. His complicated romantic relationship with Stolas, an aristocratic demon, plays a major role.

Other important characters include Millie, Moxxie, and Loona.

Major themes in the new season:

1. Millie’s pregnancy.
2. Blitzø’s red eyes (red eyes can signal true rage or are associated with hellhounds such as Loona and higher demons such as Stolas, while Blitzø is normally just an imp).
3. Loona and Octavia’s friendship.
4. Stolas and Blitzø’s relationship.
5. Stolas’s relationship with his childhood friend Vassago.

The first part of the season is planned for October: three episodes on October 14, two on October 21, and two on October 28.

### 11. Spooky Month 7 — a new episode from Sr Pelo

The seventh episode of Spooky Month, the popular animated horror series by artist Sr Pelo, was released in early October.

The main characters are two children, Skid and Pump, who love Halloween and act all year long as if the holiday never ends.

Behind the simple comedy are darker elements: cults, demons, supernatural creatures, and mysterious events.

After the new episode was released, fans began discussing its new characters, a mysterious ring, and a possible connection to Skid’s family.

The main villain of the episode is **Aspen Moor**, a creepy demonic witch who lures children into a magical world of her own creation. At first, everything looks like a cheerful fairy tale full of games and entertainment, but it gradually becomes clear that she uses illusions to distract children and steal their souls.

Her design combines a fairy-tale appearance with genuine horror. She can create hallucinations, turn events into a nightmare, and assume a frightening form. In the finale, after her talisman is destroyed, the witch’s body transforms into a tree.

### 13. Avatar: Seven Havens — a new animated series

### 33. Boisvert Analog Horror Game — a mobile game featuring Room

In 2026, the mobile horror game *Boisvert Analog Horror Game*, based on the internet-horror project *_boisvert*, became available on Android and iOS.

The game’s main monster is **Room**, a creepy black creature with long limbs and an enormous triangular head. The character is known from unusual black-and-white animations with almost no conventional dialogue, where disturbing imagery is accompanied by unsettling sounds and cryptic messages.

In the game, the player is trapped inside an abandoned house and must survive five nights. They have to monitor rooms through cameras, solve puzzles, and hide from Room as the creature becomes increasingly dangerous.

The game uses an old-VHS aesthetic, screen interference, and sudden monster appearances. Its description also mentions other characters from the Boisvert universe, including Ouriel and Dogtooth.

The game has surpassed one million installs on Google Play.

#### 2. Calm and angry trend Sprunki

Sprunki is a popular fan-made music game inspired by Incredibox, where players create melodies by combining the sounds of funny, colorful characters. Each character has a distinct name, appearance, personality, and unique sound, which helped the cast become recognizable very quickly. The game’s biggest feature is its horror mode, where the cute characters transform into disturbing creatures with distorted faces and bloody details. A large fandom has formed around Sprunki, producing animations, fan stories, alternate universes, and original character interpretations.

**Humanized characters and shipping content**

Over time, Sprunki fans began drawing the characters as humans while keeping their recognizable colors, hairstyles, accessories, and personality traits. Artists started giving them personal stories, personalities, and relationships, turning simple music-game characters into participants in romantic and dramatic plots. Shipping art and animations featuring pairs such as Gray × Wenda, Oren × Pinki, and others became especially popular, with fans often portraying the characters as being in love, jealous, or dealing with tragic events from the game’s horror version.

In this trend, one side shows a character with a “calm” meter and the other shows the same character with an “angry” meter. Their expression changes depending on the level. Different characters can be used.
